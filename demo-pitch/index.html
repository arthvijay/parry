Inside the three minutes

Every second is scored.

Live chemistry 0–100%

Callbacks, follow-up questions and local idioms push it up. Monologues and textbook stiffness drag it down.

Eye contact & smiles 250 ms

Your webcam reads smiles, nods and where you look, calibrated to your face. Look away for 4.5 s while your date talks and they call you out.

Digital wingman 4.0 s stall

Freeze and an earpiece whispers three replies (flirty, curious or playful) with English glosses.

Barge-in 120 ms

Interrupt like a human. Lip-sync stops instantly and the AI drops the rest of its sentence.

Why it's different

Not another flashcard app. A real conversation.

Language apps drill vocabulary. Parry puts you across the table from someone who listens, watches and reacts. Here are four differences.

What mattersTypical language appParry
SpeakingTap a tile, repeat a phrase, wait for a score→Full-duplex voice. Open mic, instant barge-in, under 400 ms to the date's first word
PresenceA cartoon mascot and a progress bar→Your face, in 3D. A selfie-tinted avatar mirroring your live expressions through 52 blendshapes
Social awarenessBlind to your gaze, expressions and hesitation→It reads the room. Eye contact, smiles, nods and puzzled looks, calibrated to your face
FeedbackStreaks and right/wrong answers→Live chemistry + wingman. A 0–100 meter, whispers when you stall, and a Match-or-Ghosted receipt

Built to scale

One engine. Every dialect.

Rendering, speech and persona prompting are fully separated. A new market needs zero client rewrites, only a new dialect schema.

es-ARBuenos Aires

Argentine Spanish “Che, ¿vos qué tomás?”

Enforces voseo and porteño slang (che, re copado). Penalizes stiff textbook “usted”.

es-MXCDMX · Cancún

Mexican Spanish “¿Qué onda? ¿Un cafecito?”

Rewards natural diminutives (ahorita, cafecito) and colloquialisms like qué onda and neta.

zh-CNShanghai · Beijing

Mandarin Chinese “你周末一般做什么？”

Rewards casual particles (呀, 嘛, 吧) and modern urban dating etiquette; a stiff 您 reads as distance.

hi-INMumbai · Delhi

Indian Hinglish “Main toh kaafi chill hoon”

Supports fluid Hindi–English code-switching in the same sentence. Scene kya hai?

ja-JPTokyo · Osaka

Japanese “ほんまに？”

Shifts from polite teineigo to casual tameguchi or playful Kansai-ben as chemistry rises.
